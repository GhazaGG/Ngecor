# MIX-003 PR B (#98): Tap to Collect, Hold to Stir, Repeatable Setup

## Task summary
Addressed DePo4l's re-review of PR #98 at `02eb010`: the shovel could not collect finished concrete while another batch was ready to stir, rerunning the editor setup duplicated the water drum, and the PR description test count was stale.

## Relevant previous context
- `.development-history/2026-10-10-11-10-mix003-pr98-bucket-collect-review-fixes.md`: the three bucket collect fixes, which DePo4l confirmed.
- The developer chose: at the mixing spot, a tap of R collects concrete and holding R stirs.

## Changes made
- `ShovelInput`: with an empty shovel aimed at a mixing spot, the press waits for release. Releasing before 0.25 s scoops (only finished concrete is collectable there); holding past 0.25 s stirs once, then once per 0.5 s. Everywhere else a press still scoops or dumps at once. Input handling moved to a public `ProcessUse(pressed, held, deltaTime)` so tests can drive it; `RequestUse` is now the tap action (dump or scoop).
- `ShovelAction.IsAimingAtMixingSpot` restored for that check.
- `ManualMixingSpotSetup`: one `DrumName` constant for both the spawned drum and the cleanup, so reruns replace the drum.
- Reran the setup: the committed `Dev_Ghaza` had 3 water drums from earlier setup runs; it now has 1. `ManualMixingSpot.prefab` was regenerated by the same run.
- `docs/DECISIONS.md`: records tap = collect, hold = stir at the spot, as an exception to the MAT-004 rule that holding R does not repeat shovel actions.
- Tests: stir-and-no-raw-scoop test rewritten for tap/hold; new `TapCollectsConcreteWhileAnotherBatchIsReadyToStir`.

- Hold threshold (0.25 s) and stir interval (0.5 s) are Inspector tunings on the shovel.
