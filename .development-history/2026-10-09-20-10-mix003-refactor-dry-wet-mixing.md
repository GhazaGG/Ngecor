# MIX-003 PR B: Refactor to Dry Mix, Water, Wet Mix

## Task summary
Refactored manual mixing after the developer's feel test: the spot was always sand-colored, concrete spawned as separate piles that could not be collected, and the mix was a single step. It now follows the site routine (sand and cement, dry mix, water, wet mix) and concrete is collected straight from the bed.

## Relevant previous context
- `.development-history/2026-10-09-18-56-mix003-fix-output-slots-and-held-sack.md` added bounded output slots, which this refactor removes.
- Developer decisions in this session: concrete stays in the spot, water comes from a limited drum, loading order is free but water is only accepted after the dry mix.
- Issue #55 lists water as Out of Scope; recorded as an owner-requested change in `docs/DECISIONS.md`.

## Changes made
- `ManualMixingSpot` rewritten as a phase machine: dry mix (5 actions per mixture), water accepted only after dry mix, wet mix (5 actions per batch, 1 cement + 2 sand + 1 water gives 4 concrete). Dry ingredients added after mixing need dry mixing again. Bed and progress bar change color with contents and phase. Removed output pile prefab, output point, and output slots.
- `MaterialType.Water` appended to the enum (existing serialized values unchanged). `GroundMaterialDeposit` never deposits water.
- `BulkMaterialContainer.AcceptFilter` lets an owner refuse a type in its current state (water before dry mix, concrete from outside).
- New `CollectOnlyType`: a container whose only collectable type is fixed (concrete in the spot, water in the drum); the shovel cannot scoop raw ingredients out, and the drum is bucket-only. `ShovelAction` and `BucketPourAction` use it; the bucket collect path is generalized from concrete-only.
- `ConcreteBatch` removed (deleted through `AssetDatabase.DeleteAsset` by the setup menu).
- `ManualMixingSpotSetup` rebuilds the spot prefab, creates `WaterDrum.prefab` and `Water.mat`, removes the test sack from `Dev_Ghaza`, re-places the spot, and adds a Water Drum on free floor nearby.
- Tests rewritten for the new flow; `docs/DECISIONS.md` entry updated.

## Technical decisions
- Concrete stays in the bed instead of separate pile objects, which removes the raycast placement that failed silently. MIX-002 freshness will attach to the bed's concrete.
- Water and concrete acceptance use a runtime filter on the container instead of changing generic transfer code.
- No new package, input, or networking code.
