## Technical decisions
- Tether, not a segmented rope: one max-distance joint per rope is cheaper and more stable in PhysX than a chain of joints, and it covers the most useful uses.
- Attach points only (`RopeAttachPoint`), not arbitrary colliders, which keeps the behavior predictable and testable.
- No new input binding. Interact ties and unties.
- The tool creates a believable new risk, as the project rules require: tied bodies pull each other, and a rolling-back pickup can drag a wheelbarrow or topple scaffolding. Untying is the recovery.
- Prefabs go in `Assets/Game/Prefabs/Interaction/`, because `Tools` is not a valid System in `docs/PROJECT_STRUCTURE.md`.
