## Changes made
- **PR #58 review (CHANGES_REQUESTED).**
  - Must Fix: the push uses `ForceMode.Force` from `OnControllerColliderHit`, which runs inside `Update`. Forces accumulate once per `Update` and apply at the next physics step, so push strength depends on frame rate. The fix is `ForceMode.Impulse` scaled by `Time.deltaTime`.
  - Should Fix: one test that compares displacement at 30 fps and 120 fps; rerun the suite and update the PR text, which says "not rerun".
  - Suggestions: check whether several colliders of the 9-collider wheelbarrow produce several hits per `Move`; small props under the step offset are stepped on.
- **PR #67 review (CHANGES_REQUESTED).**
  - Must Fix 1: undisclosed changes to the shared Playground scene. `Ramp` moved from (0, 1.45, 5) to (0, 1.21, 4.91). The ramp's low edge changes from about 0.22 m above the floor to flush. All 8 existing props moved about +7 to +10 on X. `Box_8` and `slop_sederhana_1` are new, and `slop_sederhana_1` is an unrotated flat slab, not a slope.
  - Must Fix 2: retest after #58 is fixed, retune `_pushStrength`, confirm three cubes on flat ground and the ramp, and revert the scene override of `_pushStrength` (1.3).
  - Should Fix: rename `WheelBarrow.prefab` to `Wheelbarrow.prefab` through the Editor; stop adding a history report per step (6 in this PR).
  - Suggestions: the wheel is a squashed capsule that behaves like a sphere and doesn't roll; the physics materials belong in `Materials/Vehicle/`, not `Settings/`.
  - Proposed acceptance: defer controlled ramp descent to VEH-002 (#14). AC #13 says "naik/turun slope", and descent under gravity was shown. This still needs the owner's confirmation.
  - Note for later tickets: constant push force makes loaded carts sluggish (about 0.9 m/s² for a 4 kg cart with 3 kg of cargo at 6.5 N), which matters for #46 (cement bags) and #14.
- **Handoff prompt on #58:** 15 ordered steps in two stages, with the frame-rate fix, the new test, retuning, retargeting #67, playtest checklist, rename, and conditions for stopping to ask.
