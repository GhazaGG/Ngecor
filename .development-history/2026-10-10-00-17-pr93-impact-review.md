## Technical decisions

The horizontal momentum check lets a fast horizontal sack impact count even when contact is on a near-horizontal support surface. Vertical-only support/fall impulses are still excluded from the impulse fallback. The ordinary 25 kg push estimate remains below 75 N·s.

- In a separate ordinary W shove pass, `CementBag_1` moved from approximately `(2.65, 1.97, -0.65)` to `(2.645, 1.97, -0.691)` along the scaffold platform. The `ScaffoldingModule_LoadTest` root remained present.

## Final result

The high-impact side throw collapsed the scaffold. The separate ordinary shove moved the sack along the scaffold platform and left the scaffold standing. The threshold change stayed limited to scaffold impact detection.
