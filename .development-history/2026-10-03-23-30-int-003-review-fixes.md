## Technical Decisions
- **Candidate-based Pinch Resolution**: When dropping while pinned against a wall, the object is squeezed along the player-obstacle axis. Instead of forcing the object forward into the obstacle or back into the capsule, `DepenetrateOnDrop` slides the object perpendicular to the obstacle normal (along the wall plane) away from the player center, then resolves obstacle clearance. This prevents explosive depenetration impulses and maintains zero wall penetration.
- **Relative Transform Computation for Compound Colliders**: For objects with multiple or offset child colliders (such as wheelbarrows or tools), PhysX `ComputePenetration` requires each collider's world pose. Calculating `colPos` and `colRot` relative to root orientation ensures compound hierarchies are accurately evaluated.
- **Zero-Distance Hit Filter in SphereCast**: When a spherecast starts with an initial overlap on an obstacle, PhysX reports a hit at distance 0 with inverse sweep normal. Clamping nearest distance to 0 collapses the hold position to player's face. Ignoring `distance <= 0.0001f` allows `ComputePenetration` to evaluate the actual 3D separating vector cleanly.
