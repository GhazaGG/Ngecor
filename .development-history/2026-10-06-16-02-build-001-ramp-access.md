# BUILD-001 Ramp Access and Load Failure Verification

## Task summary

Replaced the scaffolding's six access steps with one static wooden ramp and aligned its load threshold with the current BUILD-001 issue requirements.

## Relevant previous context

- The previous iteration changed the six access steps into thin boards.
- Issue #18 permits a static ramp or stairs, limits the player to a 0.45 m step offset and 50 degree slope, and counts cargo mass rather than CharacterController mass.
- The editor scene already contained three 25 kg cement bags on the platform and a fourth bag for overload checks.

## Changes made

- Replaced `AccessStep_1` through `AccessStep_6` with a single primitive `AccessRamp` under `Frame_Front`.
- Set the ramp to 1.0 m wide, 2.64 m long, and 0.08 m thick at a 45.5 degree slope. Reused the scaffolding wood render material and friction physics material.
- Set the load threshold to 90 kg: three cement bags total 75 kg and remain below the threshold; four total 100 kg exceed it.
- Removed CharacterController mass accounting and the unused collapse threshold. Persistent overload and hard impacts continue through the two-second wobble before the module breaks.
- Removed a redundant `Destroy(_body)` call. The script requires a Rigidbody and destroys the entire root GameObject after detaching the four parts; destroying the component separately emitted a Unity error.
- Kept `PlayerMovement.cs` and the development scene's saved layout unchanged. Restored the temporary fourth-bag test placement before saving.

## Files affected

- `Assets/Game/Prefabs/Construction/ScaffoldingModule.prefab`
- `Assets/Game/Scripts/Construction/ScaffoldingLoadFailure.cs`
- This report

## Technical decisions

- The ramp slope is below the scene player's 50 degree slope limit. The prefab uses only primitive colliders, has one Rigidbody while intact, and has no joints or MeshCollider.
- The four detached physical groups retain masses of 20 kg, 20 kg, 12.5 kg, and 12.5 kg.
- Player collision and movement code remain outside this issue's scope.

## Verification performed

- Unity 6000.3.25f1 recompile completed with `compilationFailed=false`.
- Inspected the prefab in the Editor: one `AccessRamp`, no `AccessStep` objects, BoxCollider, wood and friction materials, 90 kg load threshold, two-second wobble, and four configured break parts.
- In Play Mode, the three bags already on the deck left the intact module kinematic.
- In a separate Play Mode run, placed a fourth 25 kg bag on the platform. The intact root disappeared after overload and the four named groups each remained as a non-kinematic Rigidbody with the configured mass.
- The first overload run exposed the redundant Rigidbody-destruction error. Removed that call and repeated the overload run; the four physical parts remained without a gameplay Console error. An initial batch of premature CLI lookups logged four lookup errors; their messages were inspected and the Console was cleared after the test. The final Editor Console status reported zero errors and warnings, with `compilationFailed=false`.
- Returned the fourth bag to its original scene position and saved `Dev_DePo4l.unity`; it has no final Git diff.
- Tried simulated W input in Play Mode, but the player position did not change. Ramp traversal, walking feel, and standing jitter were therefore not verified.

## Final result

The prefab now provides a single static ramp and the load-failure script counts cargo rigidbodies only. Four-bag overload and four-part breakup were observed in Play Mode. Overall issue acceptance remains unconfirmed.

## Known limitations

- The wobble-to-break transition occurred during the overload run, but its motion and feel were not visually inspected.
- Player ascent, walking across the deck, normal contact with a support, reduced-load recovery, and hard-impact collapse were not verified.
- Keyboard simulation did not move the player; manual Game View testing is still needed.
